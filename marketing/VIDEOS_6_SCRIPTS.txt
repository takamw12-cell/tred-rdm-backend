TRED — 6 UGC-Videos (3 EN, 3 DE), 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 20–30 s
Sechs verschiedene Videos, keine Übersetzungen: jedes zeigt eine andere Funktion.
Aufbau: Hook (0–3 s) · Problem · Lösung mit echter TRED-Oberfläche · CTA.

Regeln (UWG): keine absoluten Versprechen ("bestehen", "keine Fehler", "keine
Halluzination"), kein namentlich genannter Wettbewerber, nur Funktionen, die es
in der App gibt. Jede Aussage unten ist am Code geprüft (Stand 24.11.2026);
die Zeile "Beleg" nennt die Stelle.

EN 1 — "The 11 PM Panic"                                   Feature: Panic Mode
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOOK (0–3 s)      It's 11 PM. Your exam is tomorrow. You have 80 pages left.
PROBLEM (3–7 s)   You can't read all of it tonight. And you don't know which
                  pages matter.
SOLUTION (7–20 s) Open TRED and hit Panic Mode. Pick your script. TRED pulls
                  the 3 hardest concepts and the 3 key formulas out of YOUR
                  PDF — each one with the page it comes from. Tap one, and it
                  explains it with an example from your own notes.
CTA (20–25 s)     Start free. Link in bio.
SCREEN            Dashboard → button "Panic mode: exam tomorrow?" → cheat
                  sheet with 3 concepts, 3 formulas, page numbers.
Beleg             web/components/panic-card.tsx; i18n "panic.intro".
Geändert          "the 3 exact formulas you need to pass" and "Save your grade"
                  removed: both promise an exam result.

EN 2 — "Show Me the Page"                        Feature: PDF source + SymPy
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOOK (0–3 s)      Your AI chatbot just handed you a formula. Where is it from?
PROBLEM (3–7 s)   No source. No page. You copy it into your homework and hope.
SOLUTION (7–20 s) TRED answers from your own course files and shows the page

                  the result is checked with SymPy — you see the check on
                  screen.
CTA (20–25 s)     Proof from your own PDF. Try it free.
SCREEN            Chat answer with the source chip "Skript · p. 12" → open
                  the page → solver card with the green check.
Beleg             Quellenangaben im Chat; lib/sympy-verify.ts; Probe-Karte.
Geändert          Title "The ChatGPT Lie" and the sentence "ChatGPT just made
                  up a formula" removed (named competitor, unprovable claim).
                  "TRED doesn't guess", "every calculation" and "not
                  hallucinations" removed (absolute promises).
                  "highlights the exact sentence" removed: TRED shows the
                  page; sentence highlighting is not verified in the app.
